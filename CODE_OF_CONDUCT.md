# Community Code of Conduct

Rinspace Tags welcomes technical disagreement and expects participants to keep
discussion respectful, specific and useful. Critique code, contracts, evidence
and decisions without insulting or harassing the people involved.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disclosure of private information, sexualised attention, sustained disruption,
and submitting credentials or personal data to force public handling of a
private matter.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the
project. Enforcement should consider context, document the affected project
surface, and avoid disclosing more personal information than necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to `lunifans@outlook.com`. Security reports
belong under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
